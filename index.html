Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions asked by parents and students.

What age group is eligible to join Dhananjay Classes?

Students of all ages are welcome! From primary school children (1st std onwards), 10th/12th board aspirants, engineering and medical college students, to adults and working professionals looking to refine their signature and handwriting.

Are practice materials and workbooks provided free of charge?

Yes, 100%! All course practice books, guide sheets, and customized materials are provided completely free with admission. You don't need to purchase extra books.

Can I choose my own batch timing?

Yes, absolutely. We offer complete flexibility. You can select any suitable time slot between 8:00 AM – 1:00 PM in the morning or 3:00 PM – 9:00 PM in the evening, ensuring it never clashes with your school or other coaching.

How fast can I see improvement?

Because of our 1-on-1 individual teaching method, noticeable improvement starts from the very first few sessions. We also offer a fast-track facility to complete the entire course in a minimum number of days during vacations.
